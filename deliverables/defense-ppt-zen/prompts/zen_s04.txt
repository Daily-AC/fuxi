OVERVIEW
A 16:9 minimalist editorial slide for a Chinese academic thesis defense — slide 4 of 12, listing the five engineering constraints chosen for this work. The visual anchor is a vertical numbered list of five items on the left, balanced by a single vertical ink stroke on the right. The reference image is the previous slide of the same deck — match its paper tone, deep ink black, and serif Chinese typography exactly.

STYLE
Oriental whitespace aesthetic on warm off-white paper (#FCFAF6) with subtle paper-grain texture. Monochrome deep ink black ink only — NO colored accents, NO icons, NO cartoon elements, NO rounded boxes, NO drop shadows, NO pastel palette. Mood is restrained and scholarly.

INK MARK
ONE vertical flying-white brush stroke running down the right margin of the canvas, from near top to near bottom — about 65% of slide height, 3-4% of slide width. Confident single-pass calligraphy with visible dry-brush flying-white texture. Pure deep ink black. NO second mark anywhere.

LAYOUT
A vertical enumerated list. A short slide title sits at the top-left, left-aligned. Below it, occupying the lower 70-75% of the slide, FIVE numbered items run as a vertical list in the LEFT 60% of the canvas. Each item line has THREE inline parts: a small Chinese numeral (一 / 二 / 三 / 四 / 五) in 思源宋体 Medium (slightly muted feel), then a wider gap, then a 4-6 char heading in 思源宋体 ExtraBold, then a small em-dash separator (——), then a one-line elaboration in 思源宋体 Medium. Items are separated only by line spacing (~2.0 em), with NO bullets, NO horizontal rules between items, NO boxes. The vertical ink stroke (described above) sits in the right margin, balancing the text mass on the left. NO icons.

TEXT CONTENT (each Chinese string renders EXACTLY as written, in 思源宋体 — ExtraBold for headings, Medium for numerals and elaborations; deep ink black #0E0E0E; full-width punctuation; no emoji, no decorative glyphs; em-dashes "——" must render as TWO connected long horizontal dashes):
- Slide title (top-left, ~28pt, ExtraBold, single line): "本工作的五项约束"
- Item 1 (single line, numeral ~18pt, heading ~24pt ExtraBold, body ~16pt Medium): "一  Rust 工作区　——　13 crate，编译期保证内存安全"
- Item 2 (same scale, single line): "二  本地优先　——　离线能跑，数据在用户机器上"
- Item 3 (same scale, single line): "三  非阻塞事件驱动　——　EventBus 是单一真相源（高性能基础）"
- Item 4 (same scale, single line): "四  可观测　——　所有 Agent 协作可审计可回放"
- Item 5 (same scale, single line): "五  可跨节点扩展　——　钩子化设计，单机非分布式分裂"

ACCENT
None.

WHITESPACE
At least 55% of the slide must be empty paper. The right side of the canvas (after the vertical ink stroke) and the lower-right quadrant should feel airy.

BACKGROUND
Warm off-white paper (#FCFAF6) with faint hand-made paper-grain texture and very subtle vignette toward the edges. NO landscape, NO petals, NO bamboo, NO mountains, NO scattered ink, NO clouds. The background is just paper.
